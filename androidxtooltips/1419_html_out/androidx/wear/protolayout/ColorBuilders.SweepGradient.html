The color sequence starts at the start angle and spans 360 degrees clockwise, finishing at the same angle. A color stop is a pair of a color and its offset in the gradient. The offset is the relative position of the color, beginning with 0 from the start angle and ending with 1.0 at the end angle, spanning clockwise. There must be at least 2 colors and at most 10 colors. If offset values are not set, the colors are evenly distributed in the gradient. getEndAngle Added in 1.1.0 public @ NonNull DimensionBuilders.DegreesProp   getEndAngle () Gets the end angle of the gradient, relative to the element's base angle.
