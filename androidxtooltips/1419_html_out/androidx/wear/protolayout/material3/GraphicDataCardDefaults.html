It is highly recommended for the mainContent to take a circularProgressIndicator or segmentedCircularProgressIndicator and keep its default size as expand in order to fill the available space for the best results across different screen sizes. To have contrasting content colors, when using this constructed graphic inside a graphicDataCard with one of the predefined colors in CardDefaults , it is also highly recommended to leave its ProgressIndicatorColors to default, which will automatically match to the card colors.
