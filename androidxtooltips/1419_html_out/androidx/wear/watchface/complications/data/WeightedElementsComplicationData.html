The elements field is required for this type and is expected to always be displayed. The monochromaticImage , smallImage , title , and text fields are optional but at least one of them must be set. The watch face may choose which of these fields to display, if any. If a monochromaticImage and a smallImage are both specified then only one should be displayed. If the complication is drawn with a single color it's recommended to choose monochromaticImage and apply a tint. If the complication is rendered with multiple colors it's recommended to choose the smallImage . It's best practice for a ComplicationDataSource to specify both a monochromaticImage and a smallImage .
