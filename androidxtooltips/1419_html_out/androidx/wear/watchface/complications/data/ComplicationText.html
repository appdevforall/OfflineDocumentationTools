TimeFormatComplicationText A ComplicationText that shows a formatted time. The text within a complication. This text may change over time and this interface provides both a way to determine the current text to show with getTextAt but also a way to know whether the text needs to be re-rendered, by means of returnsSameText , getNextChangeTime , and isAlwaysEmpty .
