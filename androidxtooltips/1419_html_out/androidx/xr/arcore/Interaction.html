A hit-test is a method of calculating the intersection of a ray with objects tracked by the session. Conducting a hit-test results in a list of hit objects, in other words, a hit-test does not stop at the first object hit. Returns @ NonNull List <@ NonNull HitResult > A list of HitResult objects, sorted by distance from the origin of the ray. The nearest hit is at the beginning of the list. Throws kotlin.IllegalStateException if PlaneTrackingMode is set to Disabled.
