A specific scene to render 1 or more NavEntry s. A scene instance is identified by its key and the class of the Scene , and this change drives the top-level animation based on the SceneStrategy calculating what the current Scene is for the backstack. The rendering for content should invoke the content for each NavEntry contained in entries at most once concurrently in a given Scene . It is valid for two different instances of a Scene to render the same NavEntry . In this situation, the content for a NavEntry will only be rendered in the most recent target Scene that it is displayed in, as determined by entries .
