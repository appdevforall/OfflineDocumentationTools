Whether or not the Participant is allowed to be kicked is up to the calling application, so requesting to kick a Participant may result in no action being taken. For example, the calling application may choose not to complete a request to kick the host of the call or kick the Participant representing this user. Note: This operation succeeding does not mean that the participant was kicked, it only means that the request was received and processed by the remote application. If the Participant is indeed kicked, the CallExtensionScope.addParticipantExtension onParticipantsUpdated callback will be updated to remove the kicked Participant .
