Define when activities that were launched on top should be placed adjacent to the one below, and the visual properties of such splits. Can be set either by RuleController.setRules or RuleController.addRule . The rules are always applied only to activities that will be started from the activity fills the whole parent task container or activity in the primary split after the rules were set.
