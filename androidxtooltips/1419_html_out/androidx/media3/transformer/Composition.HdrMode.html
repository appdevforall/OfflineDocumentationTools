One of HDR_MODE_KEEP_HDR , HDR_MODE_TONE_MAP_HDR_TO_SDR_USING_MEDIACODEC , HDR_MODE_TONE_MAP_HDR_TO_SDR_USING_OPEN_GL , or HDR_MODE_EXPERIMENTAL_FORCE_INTERPRET_HDR_AS_SDR . Standard Dynamic Range (SDR) input video is unaffected by these settings.
