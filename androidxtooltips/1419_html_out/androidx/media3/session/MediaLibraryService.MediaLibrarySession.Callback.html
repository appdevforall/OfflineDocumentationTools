If onGetItem returns RESULT_SUCCESS with a browsable item , the subscription is accepted and notifyChildrenChanged is immediately called with an itemCount of MAX_VALUE . In all other cases, the subscription is rejected and a result value different to RESULT_SUCCESS is returned from this method.
