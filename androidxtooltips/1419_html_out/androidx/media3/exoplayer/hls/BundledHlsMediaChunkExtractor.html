Resetting the parsing state allows support for Fragmented MP4 EXT-X-I-FRAME-STREAM-INF segments. EXT-X-I-FRAME-STREAM-INF segments are truncated to include only a leading key frame. After parsing said keyframe, an extractor may reach an unexpected end of file. By resetting its state, we can continue feeding samples from the following segments to the extractor.
