Public methods onProgress abstract void  onProgress (     long contentLength,     long bytesDownloaded,     float percentDownloaded ) Called when progress is made during a download operation. May be called directly from download , or from any other thread used by the downloader. In all cases, download is guaranteed not to return until after the last call to this method has finished executing. Parameters long contentLength The length of the content in bytes, or LENGTH_UNSET if unknown. long bytesDownloaded The number of bytes that have been downloaded. float percentDownloaded The percentage of the content that has been downloaded, or PERCENTAGE_UNSET .
