Additionally, the step is not complete until the specified time has elapsed. Time remaining after the specified distance has been completed should be spent resting. In the context of swimming, this is sometimes referred to as 'interval training'. For example, a swimming coach may specify '100m @ 1min40s'. This implies: complete 100m and if you manage it in 1min30s, you will have 10s of rest prior to the next set.
