CallEventCallback Kotlin | Java public interface CallEventCallback android.telecom.CallEventCallback CallEventCallback relays call updates (that do not require any action) from the Telecom framework out to the application. This can include operations which the app must implement on a Call due to the presence of other calls on the device, requests relayed from a Bluetooth device, or from another calling surface. These events and the associated extra keys for the Bundle parameter are mutually defined by a VoIP application and InCallService . This enables alternative calling surfaces, such as an automotive UI, to relay requests to perform other non-standard call actions to the app. For example, an automotive calling solution may offer the ability for the user to raise their hand during a meeting.
