If you are looking for a framework that sends displayable data from one app to another, consider using AppSearchManager . A SliceItem is a single unit in the tree structure of a Slice . A SliceItem a piece of content and some hints about what that content means or how it should be displayed. The types of content can be: FORMAT_SLICE FORMAT_TEXT FORMAT_IMAGE FORMAT_ACTION FORMAT_INT FORMAT_LONG FORMAT_REMOTE_INPUT FORMAT_BUNDLE The hints that a SliceItem are a set of strings which annotate the content. The hints that are guaranteed to be understood by the system are defined on Slice .
