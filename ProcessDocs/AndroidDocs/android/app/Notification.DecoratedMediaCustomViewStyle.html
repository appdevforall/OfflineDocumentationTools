Use Notification.Builder.setCustomContentView(RemoteViews) , Notification.Builder.setCustomBigContentView(RemoteViews) and Notification.Builder.setCustomHeadsUpContentView(RemoteViews) to set the corresponding custom views to display. Contrary to MediaStyle a developer has to opt-in to the colorizing of the notification by using Notification.Builder.setColorized(boolean) .
