Note that this progress is the global progress of the animation, whereas Callback.onProgress will only dispatch the insets that may be inset with WindowInsets.inset by parents of views in the hierarchy. Progress per insets animation is global for the entire animation. One animation animates all things together (in, out, ...). If they don't animate together, we'd have multiple animations. Note: In case the application is controlling the animation, the valued returned here will be the same as the application passed into WindowInsetsAnimationController.setInsetsAndAlpha(Insets, float, float) .
