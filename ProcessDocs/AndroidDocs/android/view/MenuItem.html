Constant Value Description add 10 Combines the tint and icon color and alpha channels, clamping the result to valid color values. Saturate(S + D) multiply e Multiplies the color and alpha channels of the icon with those of the tint. [Sa * Da, Sc * Dc] screen f [Sa + Da - Sa * Da, Sc + Dc - Sc * Dc] src_atop 9 The tint is drawn above the icon, but with the icon\u2019s alpha channel masking the result. [Da, Sc * Da + (1 - Sa) * Dc] src_in 5 The tint is masked by the alpha channel of the icon. The icon\u2019s color channels are thrown out. [Sa * Da, Sc * Da] src_over 3 The tint is drawn on top of the icon.
