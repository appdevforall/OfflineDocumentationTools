Criteria based APIs are deprecated because they cannot fully capture the complexity of location providers, and encourage bad development practices. Prefer to explicit select the location provider of interest ( LocationManager.FUSED_PROVIDER , etc ), rather than relying on Criteria. A class indicating the application criteria for selecting a location provider. Providers may be ordered according to accuracy, power usage, ability to report altitude, speed, bearing, and monetary cost.
