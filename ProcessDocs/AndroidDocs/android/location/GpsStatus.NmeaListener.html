Used for receiving NMEA sentences from the GPS. NMEA 0183 is a standard for communicating with marine electronic devices and is a common method for receiving data from a GPS, typically over a serial port. See NMEA 0183 for more details. You can implement this interface and call LocationManager.addNmeaListener to receive NMEA data from the GPS engine.
