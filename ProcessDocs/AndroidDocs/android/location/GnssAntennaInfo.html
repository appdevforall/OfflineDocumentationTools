GnssAntennaInfo Kotlin | Java public final class GnssAntennaInfo extends Object implements Parcelable java.lang.Object ↳ android.location.GnssAntennaInfo A class that contains information about a GNSS antenna. GNSS antenna characteristics can change with device configuration, such as when a device is folded open or closed. Antenna information is delivered to registered instances of Listener . Antenna info parameters are measured for each specific device model by the device manufacturers and provided to the Android framework. GnssAntennaInfo is returned per frequency band at the center frequency of the band.
