Rating Kotlin | Java public final class Rating extends Object implements Parcelable java.lang.Object ↳ android.media.Rating A class to encapsulate rating information used as content metadata. A rating is defined by its rating style (see RATING_HEART , RATING_THUMB_UP_DOWN , RATING_3_STARS , RATING_4_STARS , RATING_5_STARS or RATING_PERCENTAGE ) and the actual rating value (which may be defined as "unrated"), both of which are defined when the rating instance is constructed through one of the factory methods.
