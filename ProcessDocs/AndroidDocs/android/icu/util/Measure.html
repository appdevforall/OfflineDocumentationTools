An amount of a specified unit, consisting of a Number and a Unit. For example, a length measure consists of a Number and a length unit, such as feet or meters. Measure objects are parsed and formatted by subclasses of MeasureFormat. Measure objects are immutable. All subclasses must guarantee that. (However, subclassing is discouraged.)
