An instance of TimeZoneFormat works as a subformatter of SimpleDateFormat , but you can also directly get a new instance of TimeZoneFormat and formatting/parsing time zone display names. ICU implements the time zone display names defined by UTS#35 Unicode Locale Data Markup Language (LDML) . TimeZoneNames represents the time zone display name data model and this class implements the algorithm for actual formatting and parsing.
