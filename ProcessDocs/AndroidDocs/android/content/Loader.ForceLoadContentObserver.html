Use Loader.ForceLoadContentObserver An implementation of a ContentObserver that takes care of connecting it to the Loader to have the loader re-load its data when the observer is told it has changed. You do not normally need to use this yourself; it is used for you by CursorLoader to take care of executing an update when the cursor's backing data changes.
