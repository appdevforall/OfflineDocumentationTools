A mandatory stream combination refers to a specific entry in the documented sets of required stream combinations . These combinations of streams are required to be supported by the camera device. The list of stream combinations is available by invoking CameraCharacteristics.get and passing key CameraCharacteristics.SCALER_MANDATORY_STREAM_COMBINATIONS .
