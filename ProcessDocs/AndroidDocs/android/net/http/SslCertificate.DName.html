The attributes are assumed to come in most significant to least significant order which is true of human readable values returned by methods such as X500Principal.getName() . Be aware that the underlying sources of distinguished names such as instances of X509Certificate are encoded in least significant to most significant order, so make sure the value passed here has the expected ordering of attributes.
