Can be called at any time. onCanceled() will be invoked when cancellation is complete and no further callback methods will be invoked. If the request has completed or has not started, calling cancel() has no effect and onCanceled() will not be invoked. If the Executor passed in during UrlRequest construction runs tasks on a single thread, and cancel() is called on that thread, no callback methods (besides onCanceled() ) will be invoked after cancel() is called.
