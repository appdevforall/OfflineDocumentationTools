SipManager and associated classes are no longer supported and should not be used as the basis of future VOIP apps. Represents a SIP session that is associated with a SIP dialog or a standalone transaction not within a dialog. You can get a SipSession from SipManager with createSipSession() (when initiating calls) or getSessionFor() (when receiving calls).
