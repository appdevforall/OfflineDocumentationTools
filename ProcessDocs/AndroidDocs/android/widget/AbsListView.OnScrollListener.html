If the view is being scrolled, this method will be called before the next frame of the scroll is rendered. In particular, it will be called before any calls to Adapter.getView(int, View, ViewGroup) . Parameters view AbsListView : The view whose scroll state is being reported scrollState int : The current scroll state. One of SCROLL_STATE_TOUCH_SCROLL or SCROLL_STATE_IDLE .
