RoomDatabase.PrepackagedDatabaseCallback Artifact: androidx.room:room-runtime View Source Added in 2.3.0 Kotlin | Java public abstract class RoomDatabase.PrepackagedDatabaseCallback Callback for Builder.createFromAsset , Builder.createFromFile and Builder.createFromInputStream This callback will be invoked after the pre-package DB is copied but before Room had a chance to open it and therefore before the RoomDatabase.Callback methods are invoked.
