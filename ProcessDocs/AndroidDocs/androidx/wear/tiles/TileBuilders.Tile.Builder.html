The platform will attempt to refresh your tile at some point in the future after this interval has lapsed. A value of 0 here signifies that auto-refreshes should not be used (i.e. you will manually request updates via TileService#getRequester). This mechanism should not be used to update your tile more frequently than once a minute, and the system may throttle your updates if you request updates faster than this interval.
