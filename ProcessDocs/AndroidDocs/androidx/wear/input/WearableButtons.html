The location will be populated in the following manner: The provided point will be on the screen, or more typically, on the edge of the screen. The point won't be off the edge of the screen. The location returned is a screen coordinate. The unit of measurement is in pixels. The coordinates do not take rotation into account and assume that the device is in the standard upright position. Common keycodes to use are KEYCODE_STEM_PRIMARY , KEYCODE_STEM_1 , KEYCODE_STEM_2 , and KEYCODE_STEM_3 .
