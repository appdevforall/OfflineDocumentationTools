Currently, only location is supported. Use with X_KEY and Y_KEY . The key will not be present if the information is not available for the requested keycode. The location returned is a Cartesian coordinate where the bottom left corner of the screen is the origin. The unit of measurement is in pixels. The coordinates do not take rotation into account and assume that the device is in the standard upright position.
