The list will be shown below the search box, allowing users to click on individual search results. Requirements The number of items in the ItemList should be smaller or equal than the limit provided by CONTENT_LIMIT_TYPE_LIST . The host will ignore any items over that limit. The list itself cannot be selectable as set via setOnSelectedListener . Each Row can add up to 2 lines of texts via addText and cannot contain a Toggle .
