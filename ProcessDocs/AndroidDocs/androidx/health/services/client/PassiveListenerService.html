Health Services will bind to the PassiveListenerService to deliver passive monitoring updates such as data or goal updates. Clients should extend this service and override those methods of the PassiveListenerCallback that they care about. They can then pass in their service to PassiveMonitoringClient.setPassiveListenerServiceAsync to receive data updates.
