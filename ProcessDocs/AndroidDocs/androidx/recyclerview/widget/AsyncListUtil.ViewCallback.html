If there is no scroll hint, the default implementation extends the visible range by half its length in both directions. If there is a scroll hint, the range is extended by its full length in the scroll direction, and by half in the other direction. For example, if range is {100, 200} and scrollHint is HINT_SCROLL_ASC , then outRange will be {50, 300} .
