Public constructors ViewModelProvider Added in 2.2.0 public  ViewModelProvider (@ NonNull ViewModelStoreOwner  owner) Creates ViewModelProvider . This will create ViewModel instances and retain them in the ViewModelStore of the given ViewModelStoreOwner . This method will use the default factory if the owner implements HasDefaultViewModelProviderFactory . Otherwise, a NewInstanceFactory will be used.
