AnchorEntity An AnchorEntity is created to track a Pose relative to some position or surface in the "Real World." PanelEntity PanelEntity creates a spatial panel in Android XR. PerceptionSpace PerceptionSpace is ActivityPose used to track the origin of the space used by ARCore for XR APIs.
