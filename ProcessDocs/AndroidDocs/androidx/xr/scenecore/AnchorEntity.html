Children of this Entity will remain positioned relative to that location in the real world, for the purposes of creating Augmented Reality experiences. Note that Anchors are only relative to the "real world", and not virtual environments. Also, calling setParent() on an AnchorEntity has no effect, as the parenting of an Anchor is controlled by the system.
