FIT_CENTER ScaleType   ScaleType.FIT_CENTER Scale the input frames, maintaining the source aspect ratio, so it is entirely contained within the viewfinder, and center it inside the viewfinder. The background area not covered by the input frames will be black or the background color of the viewfinder. Both dimensions of the input frames will be equal or less than the corresponding dimensions of its container viewfinder.
