CameraState.ErrorType Artifact: androidx.camera:camera-core View Source Added in 1.1.0 Kotlin | Java public enum CameraState.ErrorType Types of errors the camera can encounter. CameraX tries to recover from recoverable errors, which include ERROR_CAMERA_IN_USE , ERROR_MAX_CAMERAS_IN_USE and ERROR_OTHER_RECOVERABLE_ERROR . The rest of the errors are critical, and require the intervention of the developer or user to restore camera function. These errors include ERROR_STREAM_CONFIG , ERROR_CAMERA_DISABLED , ERROR_CAMERA_FATAL_ERROR and ERROR_DO_NOT_DISTURB_MODE_ENABLED . Summary Enum Values CRITICAL An error the camera encountered that CameraX will not attempt to recover from. RECOVERABLE An error the camera encountered that CameraX will attempt to recover from.
