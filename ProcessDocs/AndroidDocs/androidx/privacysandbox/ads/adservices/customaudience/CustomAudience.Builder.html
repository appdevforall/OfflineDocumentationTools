If the field is not set, the CustomAudience will be activated at the time of joining. For example, a custom audience for lapsed users may not activate until a threshold of inactivity is reached, at which point the custom audience's ads will participate in the ad selection process, potentially redirecting lapsed users to the original owner application. The maximum delay in activation is 60 days from initial creation. If specified, the activation time must be an earlier instant than the expiration time.
