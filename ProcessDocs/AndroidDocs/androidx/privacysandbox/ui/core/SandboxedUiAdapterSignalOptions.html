The set of information that will be collected is: SandboxedSdkViewUiInfo.uiContainerWidth , SandboxedSdkViewUiInfo.uiContainerHeight , SandboxedSdkViewUiInfo.onScreenGeometry and SandboxedSdkViewUiInfo.uiContainerOpacityHint . The information that will be collected is SandboxedSdkViewUiInfo.obstructedGeometry .
