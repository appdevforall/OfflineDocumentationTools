HlsPlaylistTracker Artifact: androidx.media3:media3-exoplayer-hls View Source Kotlin | Java @ UnstableApi public interface HlsPlaylistTracker Known direct subclasses DefaultHlsPlaylistTracker DefaultHlsPlaylistTracker Default implementation for HlsPlaylistTracker . Tracks playlists associated to an HLS stream and provides snapshots. The playlist tracker is responsible for exposing the seeking window, which is defined by the segments that one of the playlists exposes. This playlist is called primary and needs to be periodically refreshed in the case of live streams. Note that the primary playlist is one of the media playlists while the multivariant playlist is an optional kind of playlist defined by the HLS specification (RFC 8216). Playlist loads might encounter errors. The tracker may choose to exclude them to ensure a primary playlist is always available.
