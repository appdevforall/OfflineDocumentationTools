Override the doWork function to do your suspending work. By default, CoroutineWorker runs on Dispatchers.Default if neither Configuration.Builder.setExecutor or Configuration.Builder.setWorkerCoroutineContext were set. A CoroutineWorker is given a maximum of ten minutes to finish its execution and return a [ListenableWorker.Result]. After this time has expired, the worker will be signalled to stop.
