BrushFamily Artifact: androidx.ink:ink-brush View Source Added in 1.0.0-alpha05 Kotlin | Java public final class BrushFamily A BrushFamily describes a family of brushes (e.g. “highlighter” or “pressure pen”), irrespective of their size or color.
