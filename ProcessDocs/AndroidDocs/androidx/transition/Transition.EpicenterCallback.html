Transitions like android.transition.Explode depend upon an epicenter for the Transition. In Explode, Views move toward or away from the center of the epicenter Rect along the vector between the epicenter and the center of the View appearing and disappearing. Some Transitions, such as android.transition.Fade pay no attention to the epicenter. Parameters @ NonNull Transition  transition The transition for which the epicenter applies. Returns @ Nullable Rect The Rect region of the epicenter of transition or null if there is no epicenter.
